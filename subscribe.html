Get updates by email

Leave your email address and you will get one email whenever there is something new: a new version of SuperOver, big news, or the Play Store launch. The email goes out on its own the moment we post the news. No ads, and you can unsubscribe any time with one tap from any email we send.

If the form above is hard to use with your screen reader, you can also open the subscribe page directly.

Your email is stored by Substack, the service that sends these updates, and is used only for SuperOver news. Read more on the privacy page.
