SuperOver 12.0: What's New

SuperOver 12.0 is our biggest update so far. It is built for blind and visually impaired cricket fans, and it works offline.

Season mode

Play a full season with a team of your own. Squads are created for you, and each player keeps a permanent identity with his batting hand and bowling style. You can pick your own eleven, your captain, vice captain and wicketkeeper. Save a match and continue later. Quick matches and Season matches have separate saves.

Coins and a daily spin

Win a match and earn coins. Win a Season and get a big bonus and a medal. Every day you get one free spin for more coins.

Better control

Player list gestures and the cursor are smoother. Pause and resume work more reliably, including long matches, and match speech is clearer.

More realism

The bat no longer passes below the pitch in new matches. Older saved matches keep their original physics.

More is coming that we are not ready to share yet. Thank you to everyone who tested and sent feedback.
