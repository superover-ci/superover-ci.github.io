Privacy

The short version: the SuperOver game works fully offline and does not collect or share any of your data. This website collects only what you choose to send us.

The game

  • SuperOver works fully offline.
  • No account, no login, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Nothing you do in the game leaves your phone.

Read the full privacy policy, in Hindi and English: SuperOver privacy policy (opens a Google Doc in a new page).

This website

  • Feedback and contact forms: what you type is sent to the team's email inbox through a form service called FormSubmit, so we can read and act on it. Your name and email are optional; give them only if you want a reply.
  • Email updates: if you subscribe, your email address is stored by Substack, the service that sends our update emails. It is used only to send you SuperOver news. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from the list.
  • WhatsApp chat: the WhatsApp link opens a chat in your own WhatsApp app; WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies there.
  • This website itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
