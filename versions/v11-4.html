Version 11.4

Folded into 11.5

Version 11.4 will not ship on its own. Everything in it, plus more fixes and new features, ships as Version 11.5.

Version 11.4 was the next update. It is built and has been checked thoroughly on the computer, ball by ball. It has not yet been tested on a real phone. This is what is in it:

  • The whole game has been rebuilt. Every screen now runs directly on your phone. There is no web view inside the game any more.
  • Meet the commentators. In English, Max and Blake call your match. In Hindi, Veer and Arjun call it. When a match begins, the commentators introduce themselves before the first over.
  • Meet the umpires. Every match has two on-field umpires and a TV umpire. Matches hosted in India have Indian umpires: Ravi Mehra and Kunal Sethi on the field, and Nitin Rao on TV. Everywhere else, Aaron Cole and Ethan Blake are on the field and Nathan Reed is on TV. Before the toss, the umpire at the bowling end introduces himself, and after every over he tells you which end he has moved to.
  • Clearer reviews. A review now goes straight to the TV umpire, with new recorded speech. He says what is being reviewed and what the original decision was, then takes you through each check. The wording varies from review to review.
  • Score summary in both languages. At the end of every over, and at the innings break, the game speaks a short score summary in your chosen commentary language, English or Hindi.
  • Music keeps playing. Menu music no longer stops when the game speaks or when TalkBack is on. It dips softly only while the game itself is speaking, then returns to full volume.
  • Commentary fixes. Player arrival commentary now speaks in Hindi when Hindi is chosen. The outside-leg wide line is now its own clear sentence.
  • Fair computer play. The computer now thinks and chooses with the same time and the same choices as you. Neither side gets an unfair advantage.
  • Saved games. Matches saved in version 11.3 or older could not be carried into this version; the game announces this on first launch. Every match saved from this version onward will keep opening in all future versions.
  • Player names. 203 names use new first-attempt recordings that no one has checked by ear yet. All names stay in the game. If a name sounds wrong, report it through the Feedback page and it will be fixed in a later update.
