Become a beta tester

SuperOver grows one version at a time, and every version is shaped by its testers. Testers try new features first and tell us what works, what is confusing, and what they would love to hear next. If that sounds like you, leave your details below and we will be in touch.











Terms and Conditions for testers

  1. Test versions of SuperOver are works in progress. They may have bugs, and features may change or be removed before the public release.
  2. Test builds are for you only. Please do not share a build, or your access to it, with anyone without our permission.
  3. Your feedback, ideas and reports may be used to improve SuperOver.
  4. Your details are used only to contact you about testing SuperOver. They are not shared with anyone else.
  5. Testing is voluntary and unpaid. There is no payment or other compensation unless we agree something different with you.
  6. You can stop testing at any time, and we can end a place in the tester programme at any time.

If the form is hard to use with your screen reader, you can also message us on WhatsApp.
