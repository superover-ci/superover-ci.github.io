What is SuperOver

SuperOver is a full cricket game for the blind community. Every menu is spoken, a commentator describes every ball, and you bat, bowl and field with swipes. It works with TalkBack on or off, needs no account and no internet, and collects no personal data.

What is new in 12.0

  • Season mode: take a team through a whole Season, pick your eleven, captain and wicketkeeper, and save and continue later.
  • Coins, a free spin every day, and a medal for winning a Season.
  • Clearer match speech, more reliable pause and resume, and smoother player lists.
  • Fixes for problems our testers reported.

How to install, step by step

  1. Press Download. The Download button is further down this page, under "Download SuperOver 12.0".
  2. Keep the file. Your phone or browser may ask "Keep this file?" or "Download anyway?". That question comes from the phone, because the app comes from outside the Play Store. Choose Keep or Download.
  3. Open the file. When the download finishes, open the notification, or open the Downloads folder or the Files app and double tap SuperOver-12.0.apk.
  4. Allow the install if asked. If the phone says it is not allowed to install apps from this browser, double tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  5. Install. Double tap Install. If Google Play Protect says it has not seen this app before, choose More details, then Install anyway.
  6. Open the game. When it says the app is installed, double tap Open. It works with TalkBack on or off.

Please download SuperOver only from this website. Do not install a file with this name from anywhere else.

Good to know

  • This is the first public release. It was tested by a group of blind players, but you may still find bugs. Please tell us.
  • The game is not on Google Play yet.
  • If you already have an earlier SuperOver test version, 12.0 installs over it as an update. Do not uninstall first.
  • It is a large file. Use Wi-Fi if you can, and keep free space on your phone.
