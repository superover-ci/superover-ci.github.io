Version 12.0

Coming soon

Version 12.0 is the next big step for SuperOver. It brings all the recent improvements together in one update, and the main focus is sound. We are still building and testing it carefully, so it will be released only when it is ready. Here is everything we are working on for it.

The sound of 12.0

  • A new commentary voice. We are working on a new English commentary voice that sounds far more natural and alive, so a match feels closer to listening to a real broadcast.
  • A Hindi commentary voice. Also in progress: a Hindi voice with the same natural feel, for players who prefer to play in Hindi.
  • Sharper commentary with punchlines. More expressive lines for fours, sixes, wickets and the tense final overs, in English and Hindi, so the commentator reacts the way a real one does when the match is on the line.
  • Commentary at the end of every over. When an over finishes, the commentator sums it up, with 16 different variations so it does not sound repeated.
  • A livelier crowd. The crowd sounds wider and fuller, and it rises on fours and sixes, so the stadium feels alive around you.
  • Clearer match sounds. A louder bat sound and three different wicket sounds, so you always know what happened by ear alone.
  • Stronger 3D sound, pitch and weather. Sound placed more clearly around you, plus pitch and weather conditions that change how a match plays, so no two matches feel the same.
  • Fielders by name. When a fielder stops or catches the ball, you hear who it was, so following the play is easy.

The game itself

  • Umpire review for close decisions. On a close call, a review can be taken, with a 10-second timer to decide, just like the reviews in a real match.
  • Official playing rules. 12.0 follows ICC playing conditions more closely, so the game behaves the way real cricket does.
  • Rain handled fairly. If rain stops play, the target is revised with our own clearly labelled method. It is not the official DLS method, and the game tells you so.
  • Bowler figures and over limits. You can hear every bowler's overs, runs and wickets. And as in real cricket, a bowler can bowl at most 4 overs in a 20-over match, 2 in a 10-over match and 10 in a 50-over match.
  • Deeper strategy. Bowling changes, field choices and batting plans matter more, so thinking like a captain is rewarded.

Controls

  • A cleaner Gestures mode. Gestures and buttons stay out of each other's way, so a swipe does what you meant it to do. And in Gestures mode, a two-finger single tap reads out both batters' runs and balls.

Fixes

  • 12.0 fixes the freeze and the blank screen that some players hit, along with many smaller bugs reported by our testers.

We have a few more surprises that we are keeping for the launch. If we get the permissions in time, the plan is to release 12.0 before 1 January 2027.
