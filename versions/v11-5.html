Version 11.5

Folded into 11.6.2

Version 11.5 is no longer the current version. Its changes live on in Version 11.6.2. It kept everything that was planned for 11.4 and 11.4.1, with nothing changed and nothing removed.

New in 11.5

  • Smarter computer play, kept exactly as it was set. The computer's batting and bowling use the AI prediction tuning between your players and the computer's players, with no changes.
  • Feedback and What's New open this website. In the game, tapping What's New opens this page, and tapping Feedback opens the feedback form, so you can report a problem or send an idea straight from the game.

Everything from 11.4 and 11.4.1

All the features and fixes prepared for 11.4 and 11.4.1 are in 11.5. The main ones:

  • New commentary. In English, Max and Blake call your match. In Hindi, Veer and Arjun call it. When a match begins, the commentators introduce themselves before the first over. Player arrival commentary speaks in Hindi when Hindi is chosen.
  • Every match has two on-field umpires and a TV umpire. Before the toss, the umpire at the bowling end introduces himself, and after every over he tells you which end he has moved to.
  • Clearer reviews. A review goes straight to the TV umpire, with new recorded speech, and the wording varies from review to review.
  • Score summary in your chosen commentary language at the end of every over and at the innings break.
  • Menu music keeps playing. It dips softly only while the game itself is speaking, then returns to full volume, even when TalkBack is on.
  • Fair computer play. The computer thinks and chooses with the same time and the same choices as you.
  • All 12 teams are in the team list, and the Continue and Auto Select buttons are back on the Playing 11 screen.
  • Saved playing 11 prefill. When you pick a team you have played with before, its saved playing 11 is filled in for you. You can still change it before the match.
  • Stereo placement setting. When it is on, crowd cheers and clapping come from the left or the right, and the glove sound and the ball bounce follow the ball.
  • Spoken screen prompts are back, and a Privacy Policy screen has been added inside the game.
  • Credits notes on the About screen, so you can read who made each part of the game.
  • Saved games. Matches saved in version 11.3 or older cannot be carried over; the game announces this on first launch. Every match saved from this version onward will keep opening in all future versions.
  • Player names. If a name sounds wrong, report it through the feedback form and it will be fixed in a later update.

The full lists are on the Version 11.4 and Version 11.4.1 pages.
