SuperOver: a cricket game you play with your ears

SuperOver is an Android cricket game made for blind and visually impaired players. It works fully offline, speaks everything it does, and is played with simple swipes. No eyes needed.

Become a beta tester See what's new Get updates by email

Why SuperOver

Made for blind players

Every menu is spoken, every ball is described by a commentator, and every shot and delivery is chosen by gesture. You can play a full match without seeing the screen, with TalkBack on or off.

Fully offline

No account, no login, and no internet needed. The game collects no personal data. Play anywhere, any time.

A real stadium in your pocket

Hindi and English commentary, crowd noise, and the sound of bat, ball and stumps, all recorded and tuned with feedback from blind testers.

Where the game is today

SuperOver is being tested right now with a beta group of blind players, and every update goes through their feedback before the next one. The Google Play launch is planned for 1 January 2027. There is no public download yet.
