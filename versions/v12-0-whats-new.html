SuperOver 12.0: What's New

Version 12.0

SuperOver 12.0 is our biggest update so far. SuperOver is a cricket game you play with your ears, made for blind and visually impaired fans, and it works offline. This page lists what is new, what we fixed, and where we are heading next.

Season mode

  • Play a full Season. Take a team of your own through a whole Season, one match at a time.
  • Squads made for you. Each squad is created for you. Every player keeps a permanent identity, with his own batting hand and bowling style.
  • Your team, your choices. Pick your own eleven, your captain, your vice captain and your wicketkeeper.
  • Save and continue. Save a match and carry on later. Quick matches and Season matches have separate saves, so one never overwrites the other.

Coins and a daily spin

  • Earn coins. Win a match and you earn coins. Coins are shared across the game.
  • Season rewards. Win a Season and you get a big bonus and a medal.
  • One free spin a day. Every day you get one free spin for more coins.

More realism

  • The bat stays above the pitch. In new matches the bat no longer passes below the pitch. Older saved matches keep their original physics.

Better control and clearer speech

  • Smoother player list. Gestures and the cursor in the player list work more smoothly.
  • More reliable pause and resume. Pause and resume work better, including in long matches.
  • Clearer match speech. Match calls are spoken more clearly and consistently.

Fixed, thanks to our testers

These problems were reported by our testers. They are fixed in 12.0.

  • A batter who was out could return to the crease.
  • Pausing a 50-over match did not work properly.
  • Some deliveries were not announced by speech.
  • The resume gesture did not always work.
  • The cursor in the player list jumped to the wrong place.

What comes next

We keep working on the sound of the game, commentary and the match experience. More updates will follow, and we will share them on this page as they are ready. Thank you to everyone who tested and sent feedback.

Found a bug, or have an idea that would make SuperOver better? Send feedback. Every report is read.
