SuperOver: a cricket game you play with your ears

Bat, bowl and field with simple swipes while a commentator describes every ball. Made for blind and visually impaired players. No eyes needed.

Why SuperOver

Made for blind players

Every menu is spoken, every ball is described, and every shot and delivery is chosen by gesture. Play a full match without seeing the screen, with TalkBack on or off.

Fully offline

No internet and no login. Play anywhere, any time. The game collects no personal data.

A stadium in your ears

Hindi and English commentary, crowd noise, and the sound of bat, ball and stumps, tuned with feedback from blind testers.

Season mode

Take a team through a whole Season, pick your eleven, earn coins, and save and continue later.

How it works

  1. Download and install The Download page explains each step for TalkBack users.
  2. Pick a match Choose a Quick Match or a Season, your team and your overs. Menus are spoken to you.
  3. Play by swipe and sound Listen to the bowler and the commentary, then swipe to play your shot or choose your delivery.

New in 12.0

  • Season mode: pick your eleven, captain and wicketkeeper, and carry on later.
  • Coins, a free spin every day, and a medal for winning a Season.
  • Clearer match speech and more reliable pause and resume.
  • Fixes for problems our blind testers reported.
