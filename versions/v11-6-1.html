Folded into 11.6.2

Version 11.6.1 is no longer the current version. Its changes live on in Version 11.6.2.

New in 11.6.1

  • Menu and match setup screens now have their own tap and swipe sounds. Match play stays free of them.
  • The old partner calls between batters have been removed.
  • Five players who were missing their catch-fielder calls now have them.
  • The bat sound on the last ball of an innings is no longer dropped.
  • Review sounds now follow the sound mute setting.
