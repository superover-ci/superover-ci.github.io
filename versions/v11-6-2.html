Version 11.6.2

Current test version

Version 11.6.2 is the current test version of SuperOver. It keeps everything from versions 11.5.1, 11.6 and 11.6.1, and adds more reliable saving, new drum sounds and full sound credits.

New in 11.6.2

  • Saving and resuming a match is more reliable, and the game recovers better if a save goes wrong.
  • New drum variations in the stadium, and a short drum roll for big decisions.
  • Full sound credits now live on the About screen, so you can read who made every sound in the game.

What came in 11.6 and 11.6.1

  • A smarter opponent on new matches. The computer now reads the game: it chooses its bowling from the shots you have played, sets its field for the match situation, and picks its own shots with a plan. The same rules govern both teams. Matches you saved before keep playing the way they always did.
  • A fair toss. The computer now calls heads or tails too, and wins its share.
  • A fresh ground every match. The same ground will not host two of your matches in a row.
  • Every boundary gets its drum, even when a four or six ends an innings, wins the match, or brings up a hundred.
  • Clearer opening moments. The opening batters are introduced as the striker and the non-striker, and the umpire's positions are explained once, at the start of the match.
  • Menu and setup screens have their own tap and swipe sounds, while match play stays free of them.
  • The bat sound on the last ball of an innings is no longer dropped, five players who were missing their catch-fielder calls have them back, and review sounds now follow the mute setting.
  • Fixes for the bowling controls, the appeal sound, interrupted commentary, and a rare freeze in old saved matches.

The details are on the Version 11.6 and Version 11.6.1 pages.
