About SuperOver

Most cricket games ask you to watch the screen. SuperOver does not. It is a full cricket game built so that a blind player hears everything and sees nothing at all.

Why it exists

SuperOver started from a simple gap: there is no complete cricket game made for the blind community. Sighted players have dozens of cricket games. Blind players had none that took them seriously, so we are building one, tested at every step by blind players themselves.

What you can do

  • Play a Quick Match in T20, ODI and other overs formats.
  • Bat, bowl and field using swipe gestures, with a gesture guide inside the game.
  • Pick your team, playing eleven, wicketkeeper and bowler each over.
  • Take reviews with DRS, hear the third umpire, and get a match summary at the end.
  • Hear Hindi or English commentary, crowd noise, and the sound of bat, ball and stumps.
  • Save a match and resume it later.
  • Play with TalkBack on using buttons, or with TalkBack off using the game's own spoken menus and gestures.

Good to know

  • No account, no login, no ads at the moment.
  • Fully offline. The game collects no personal data.
  • For Android 7 and above.
  • It is being tested with a group of blind players before the Google Play launch planned for 1 January 2027.

Who is behind it

SuperOver is planned by Digital Site Foundation and built together with a beta group of blind players who test every version and shape what comes next. The sounds come from free, properly licensed sources, with each creator credited inside the game.
